**Starfall Duel** — an original turn-based card duel of the four suits (Ember, Tide, Leaf, Star). You face Lyra, Star Herald, across a starlit table: 40 life each, energy that grows every turn, and a 30-card deck of 10 original cards — strikes, shields, heals and draws. Play cards with mouse or keyboard (1–7 to play, E to end turn), watch your shield soak Lyra's strikes, and be the first to bring her to 0 life. Title screen, live table with both hands, readable card values and suits, visible life totals, and win and loss screens — all in plain static files, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Or open `game/index.html` directly — the game needs nothing but the files in `game/`.
